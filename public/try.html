One recording, heard four ways. Keep the best words.

Hold the button, or the space bar, and say a sentence with a name or a technical term in it. When you let go, your clip goes to AssemblyAI four ways at once. Below you'll see what each lane heard, where they disagreed, and what the vote decided.

Sample mode: this won't hear you. The server was started without an AssemblyAI key, so every recording returns the same captured sample, not what you said. Restart it with ASSEMBLYAI_API_KEY=… npm start to transcribe your own voice.
